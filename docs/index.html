GridWave – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

GridWave ("the app") is a music-making app. This policy explains what happens to information when you use it.

Information the developer collects

The developer does not collect, receive or store any personal information. There are no accounts, and your music is never uploaded to the developer.

Your audio and songs

Audio files you import or record, and songs you save, are processed and stored only on your device (inside the app's local storage). If you export a WAV, MP3 or MIDI file, it is shared with the app you choose. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes your saved songs.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only when you tap Record Mic. Recordings stay on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location, device and app information and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) the app shows Google's consent form so you can choose how your data is used. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
